# Multi-Evidence Presentation Boundary v1

## Core rule
Multiple Evidence items supporting one Fact/Event/Assessment are additional provenance, not duplicate truths to be silently collapsed or multiplied.

## Product meaning
- one shared claim may have one or many supporting Evidence items;
- identical claim value with additional valid Evidence strengthens provenance coverage, not numeric weight;
- different Evidence must remain individually inspectable by source, identity, period/date, and locator;
- Evidence count alone is not confidence, importance, or correctness.

## Conflict rule
If supporting Evidence disagrees on the claim value/meaning, do not summarize it as consensus. Preserve the conflict state and expose the disagreement.

## Presentation restraint
Do not show the same Fact/Event repeatedly merely because it has multiple Evidence links. Show one claim with its evidence trail unless the user opens provenance details.

## Implementation boundary
Aggregation queries, evidence drawers, badges, confidence computation, and tests are CODEX-FIRST/later work.
